Frequently Asked Questions

Is BrainScan AI a medical diagnosis system?

No. BrainScan AI is an AI-assisted educational and decision-support project. Medical decisions should be made by qualified healthcare professionals.

What AI model does it use?

The planned primary model is ResNet50 using transfer learning for MRI image classification.

What is Grad-CAM?

Grad-CAM is an explainability technique that visualizes image regions that contributed to a neural network's prediction.
